Scope: EKM single-page analyzer (Your data, Clean & enhance, Analyze, Charts). Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: researchers and analysts turning a raw table into findings they can defend.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every result is presented like a conference poster panel, method beside figure beside conclusion, so a reading is never separated from how it was made. Refuses the grey SaaS dashboard of loose metric cards.
OWN-WORLD: User-pinned chalkboard: a lecture hall. One dark world for every viewer (user-pinned): the slate-green board with chalk-white text and coloured chalk (yellow highlight, blue links, pink and green series); no light theme. Poster structure on the board: a darker ledge band with the tabs, ruled panels numbered 1-3. STIX Two Text for headings and typeset formulas (the Method panel writes the actual formula), Archivo for controls and figures. No wood frame, chalk-dust textures or handwriting fonts.
STORY: The analyst loads data, sees its health, cleans it with an undoable history, then reads each analysis left to right: what was done, what it shows, what it means.
